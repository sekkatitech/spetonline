
13-inch MacBook Neo

MacBook Neo. Amazing Mac. Surprising price.

MacBook Neo flies through everyday business tasks like tracking inventory, updating your website and creating polished presentations. With a durable aluminium design, a brilliant Liquid Retina display, the A18 Pro chip built for AI and Apple Intelligence1, up to 16 hours of battery life2 and four stunning colours — it’s an amazing Mac for business, at a surprising price.

Features:

DESIGNED TO GO THE DISTANCE — With an incredibly durable aluminium design, MacBook Neo is built to last. It’s small, compact and lightweight — ready to go wherever your business takes you.
ROCK-SOLID RELIABILITY — Free, automatic operating system updates run in the background to keep your Mac running fast and smooth — now and into the future.
A BRILLIANT 13-INCH DISPLAY3 — Feast your eyes on the gorgeous Liquid Retina display and do your best work in pixel-perfect clarity. A 2408x1506 resolution, up to 500 nits of brightness and support for one billion colours provide vivid images and crisp text.
POWER FOR EVERYDAY BUSINESS TASKS — MacBook Neo with the A18 Pro chip delivers the performance and AI capabilities businesses need to get things done. Whether you’re building spreadsheets or managing payroll, everything just flows.
UP TO 16 HOURS OF BATTERY LIFE2 — With all-day battery life, MacBook Neo powers through full workdays — whether you’re at the office, travelling or working remotely.
LOOK AND SOUND GREAT — MacBook Neo comes with a 1080p FaceTime HD camera, a dual-mic array to enhance your voice for crystal-clear calls, and two side-firing speakers for immersive sound with Spatial Audio. Perfect for client calls, team meetings and presentations.
MAGIC KEYBOARD WITH TOUCH ID OPTION — Employees get a precise and comfortable typing experience with Magic Keyboard. And the large Multi-Touch trackpad lets you tap, pinch, swipe and click anywhere. Plus the MacBook Neo model with Touch ID uses your fingerprint to unlock your Mac, authorise payments, and sign in to apps and sites.
BUILT FOR AI — Apple silicon, and every major component that powers it, is designed to efficiently run on-device AI activities. And Apple Intelligence helps you write, express yourself and get things done effortlessly with groundbreaking privacy protections at every step1.
IF YOU LOVE IPHONE, YOU’LL LOVE MAC — Mac works like magic with your other Apple devices. View and control what’s on your iPhone from your Mac with iPhone Mirroring4. Copy something on iPhone and paste it on Mac.
CONNECT IT ALL — MacBook Neo features two USB-C ports and a headphone jack for connecting accessories, transferring data and charging. And it supports Wi-Fi 6E and Bluetooth 6.0 to keep you wirelessly connected.
SECURE BY DESIGN — Apple silicon works with macOS to deliver advanced hardware and software security. And the Secure Enclave is designed to protect sensitive data such as encryption keys and biometrics by isolating them from the main processor.
POWER ADAPTERS — Please be aware: MacBook Neo comes with a USB C charging cable, but does not include a power adapter. A USB C power adapter or other USB PD power source that can provide 20W or higher is required to charge this device. For optimal charging, Apple recommends pairing this Mac with the Apple 20W USB C Power Adapter.

MacBook Air 15″. Might takes flight.

MacBook Air with the M5 chip brings blazing speed and powerful AI capabilities into an incredibly portable design. With Apple Intelligence1, up to 18 hours of battery life2 and fast SSD storage starting with 512GB, you can work, create and play anywhere life takes you.

SUPERCHARGED BY M5 — With its faster CPU and unified memory, M5 delivers even more performance and fluidity across apps, making multitasking and creative workflows smooth and responsive. A powerful Neural Engine and next-generation GPU with Neural Accelerators give you a powerful platform for AI.
APPLE INTELLIGENCE — Apple Intelligence is the personal intelligence system that helps you write, express yourself and get things done effortlessly. With groundbreaking privacy protections, it gives you peace of mind that no one else can access your data — not even Apple1.
UP TO 18 HOURS OF BATTERY LIFE2 — MacBook Air delivers incredible battery life with amazing performance, so you can power through a full day of work or class without worrying about plugging in.
A BRILLIANT 15.3 INCH DISPLAY3 — The Liquid Retina display supports 1 billion colours. Photos and videos pop with rich contrast and sharp detail, and text appears super crisp.
12MP CENTER STAGE CAMERA — Automatically stay in frame during video calls with Centre Stage, or share a top-down view of your workspace with Desk View. And with a three-mic array and six-speaker sound system with Spatial Audio and Dolby Atmos, everything sounds great. 
CONNECT IT ALL — MacBook Air features two Thunderbolt 4 ports, a MagSafe charging port, a headphone jack — as well as the Apple N1 wireless chip for Wi Fi 74 and Bluetooth 6. And it supports up to two external displays.
MACOS RUNS APPS FAST — All your go-to apps run lightning fast in macOS, including built-in apps like FaceTime4 and Messages. Plus, built-in virus protection and free software updates help keep your Mac running smoothly and securely.
IF YOU LOVE IPHONE, YOU’LL LOVE MAC — Mac works like magic with your other Apple devices. View and control what’s on your iPhone from your Mac with iPhone Mirroring6. Copy something on iPhone and paste it on Mac. Send texts with Messages from your Mac, or use your Mac to answer FaceTime calls5.
POWER ADAPTERS — Please be aware: MacBook Air (15 inch) comes with a USB C to MagSafe 3 charging cable, but does not include a power adapter. A USB C power adapter or other USB PD power source that can provide 35W or higher is required to charge this device. For optimal charging, Apple recommends pairing this Mac with the Apple 35W Dual USB C Port Power Adapter, or the 70W USB C Power Adapter to take advantage of fast charge (charging up to 50% in 35 minutes) 7.
