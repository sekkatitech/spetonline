## 7. RETURNS & REFUNDS

### 7.1 Right to Return
Under the **Consumer Protection Act (CPA)** and the **ECT Act**, you can return goods if:
- The item is **defective, damaged, or not as described**
- You received the **incorrect product**
- The product is returned within **7 days of delivery** in its original packaging, unused and in resalable condition

### 7.2 Return Process
1. Contact us via **email or phone within 7 days** of receiving your order
2. You'll receive a **Return Reference Number (RRN)** with instructions
3. Returns are processed in coordination with our supply chain partners
4. SPET Online manages and facilitates the entire return process on your behalf

### 7.3 Non-Returnable Items
- Software, digital licences, or opened consumable products
- Items damaged due to misuse, negligence, or unauthorised modification
- Products returned without original packaging or accessories

### 7.4 Refunds
- Approved refunds are processed within **7–14 business days**
- Refunds are issued via the **original payment method**
- Shipping costs are **non-refundable** unless the return is due to our error

### 7.5 Cooling-Off Period
Under **Section 44 of the ECT Act**, you have the right to cancel any electronic purchase within **7 days of receiving the goods**, without reason, at your own cost for return shipping, provided the product is unused and in its original packaging.

## 12. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

### Ordering & Payments
**Q: How do I place an order?**  
A: Browse our catalogue, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You will receive a confirmation email once your order is placed.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**  
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, EFT (Bob Pay instant and manual), Capitec Pay, ABSA Pay, PayShap, Apple Pay, and Google Pay — all via the PayFast secure gateway.

**Q: Is my payment information secure?**  
A: Yes. All transactions are processed through encrypted payment gateways. SPET Online does not store your card details.

**Q: Do I need an account to checkout?**  
A: Yes. You need to create an account and verify your email before you can place an order.

### Products & Stock
**Q: Are all products available in stock?**  
A: We work with verified technology partners to ensure availability. If an item is out of stock after your order, we will notify you immediately and offer an alternative or full refund.

**Q: Do products come with a warranty?**  
A: Yes. All products carry the manufacturer's warranty as specified per product listing.

**Q: Are prices negotiable?**  
A: Retail prices are fixed. For bulk/enterprise orders, dedicated pricing is available through the Enterprise Portal.

### Shipping & Delivery
**Q: Where does SPET Online deliver?**  
A: We deliver nationwide across all 9 provinces in South Africa.

**Q: How long does delivery take?**  
A: Typically 3–7 business days from order confirmation, depending on your location and product availability.

**Q: Is there free shipping?**  
A: Yes! Orders over R2,500 qualify for free delivery nationwide.

**Q: How do I track my order?**  
A: Once dispatched, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can also check order status in your account dashboard.

**Q: How much is shipping for orders under R2,500?**  
A: R90 flat rate for packages ≤5kg. For heavier packages, the rate is calculated via The Courier Guy at checkout.

### Returns & Refunds
**Q: What if I receive a damaged or incorrect item?**  
A: Contact us within 7 days with your order number and a photo. We will facilitate a replacement or refund as quickly as possible.

**Q: How long does a refund take?**  
A: Approved refunds are processed within 7–14 business days via your original payment method.

**Q: Can I return a product if I changed my mind?**
